# Assembly interference and boundary distance

`tcad.assembly_inspection` contract 1 stores inspection intent on the owning assembly. Its report is disposable evaluation data with independent report version 2. Saved definitions remain at contract version 1 without migration. Version-1 reports are rejected as stale and regenerated; version 2 adds the distinct native contact and disjoint-interior statuses. Semantic inspection metadata exposes both `version` and `report_version`. Each target saves the complete root-relative leaf occurrence path and its exact expected leaf configuration; equal definition names or repeated component instances are never interchangeable. Missing, suppressed, ambiguous or configuration-changed targets remain explicit inactive entries. Reordering the selected scope preserves an unordered pair's saved result identity, while first/second witnesses retain report order.

An inspection may follow every owning assembly configuration or a particular named configuration. Leaf configuration identities remain explicit in both modes. The docked editor can bind to the current named configuration or remove that binding. Editing captures the current root configuration, and switching it requires finishing or cancelling that edit. The initial scope contains up to 64 active leaves, with explicit notice when a larger assembly requires scope selection. Up to 32 inspections can be saved per assembly. Open the owning assembly as the document root to edit or inspect its report.

## Native interference

Interference uses the actual evaluated configured native solids and mate-solved rigid, right-handed occurrence placements. Exploded views and cached history presentations never affect the calculation. Open surfaces participate in boundary distance but do not establish volumetric interference.

For a selected body pair, conservative native trimmed-carrier bounds can establish axis separation with a margin based on the authored Boolean tolerance and coordinate magnitude. Before constructing a common solid, inspection also searches native planar support faces for a separating plane. Every complete carrier of both bodies must lie in opposite closed halfspaces. The proof uses conservative native enclosures where sufficient and exact dyadic arithmetic on the original positive homogeneous controls at equality. A floating-point normal or a divided rational control never decides the sign. The authored Boolean tolerance does not widen this separator: even a small proven penetration cannot be called contact.

A strict separator reports native separation. A weak separator establishes disjoint interiors, but by itself does **not** establish touching. Touching additionally requires an exact shared boundary witness from coplanar native trimmed faces. Witnesses come from intersecting native line segments or an exact curve endpoint lying in the other trimmed face. Winding includes every outer/inner loop, so a point in a hole is excluded. Curve-to-chord winding reduction is allowed only when a positive native control hull excludes the witness; single rational Bernstein spans use exact half subdivision when needed. Winding requires exact carrier endpoint closure. A tolerance-stitched loop cannot supply this proof, though an individually admitted native boundary segment can still establish contact with another admitted segment or closed region.

The resulting statuses are distinct:

- `separated_native_hulls`: conservative native hulls or exact supporting planes establish a strict gap; no minimum-distance value is asserted.
- `touching_native_boundaries`: all body pairs have disjoint interiors, and at least one shared native boundary point is proven. Engineering clearance is zero.
- `disjoint_native_interiors`: all body pairs have disjoint interiors, but positive separation versus boundary contact remains unresolved. No clearance value is supplied.
- `validated_common_solid`: a nonempty native Boolean common passes closed, oriented B-rep and trim validation; engineering clearance is zero.
- `no_common_solid_returned` or `unavailable`: missing common output or a failure does not certify separation or contact.

Support proofs cover stored native line, polynomial and positive rational controls, and planar parameter curves. Conservative complete sphere/torus carrier enclosures can participate when sufficient. Planar boundary hulls require exact membership in their own plane before either axis-separation or support proofs; a modeling-tolerance residual cannot certify a halfspace. Revolution carriers stay on the Boolean path because their trim-dependent axial domain requires additional exact admission. Unsupported carriers, unresolved winding, nonplanar tangencies and exhausted budgets retain an unresolved result or continue to the existing native Boolean path. These bounded support/contact predicates are exact with respect to the evaluated native binary64 definitions; they do not convert the complete kernel or Boolean construction to exact arithmetic.

When no support proof settles a pair, the native Boolean common is constructed at the saved tolerance and checked as a closed, oriented B-rep with consistent boundaries. Nonempty validated common results report native interference and zero engineering clearance. The result counts body-pair common outputs, rather than pretending to count union-connected overlap regions. An empty common result still does not certify separation or settle coincident/tangent contact. Backend errors, invalid common results, unsupported carrier preflight and exhausted budgets remain explicit uncertainty.

An optional common-volume estimate is available only for a single complete body-pair common result with planar faces and straight boundary edges. A shifted-origin compensated signed-boundary integral handles oriented outer loops, holes and cavities without invoking an unbounded tessellator. It has no numerical error certificate. Curved common results omit volume. Multiple body-pair common results may overlap, so their volumes are not summed into a misleading union volume.

## Approximate boundary distance

Minimum boundary distance uses the currently evaluated f64 boundary triangles after applying assembled occurrence placements. A bounded spatial hierarchy searches vertex/triangle, edge/edge and edge/triangle intersection candidates. The returned value and its two witnesses are explicitly approximate; no native tessellation-deviation or numerical error bound is available. The requested clearance comparison describes this boundary estimate and is not a native clearance certificate.

Containment can have a positive boundary distance while native volumetric interference establishes zero engineering clearance. Reports keep those two quantities distinct. Faces in witness records retain application-owned output owners and construction references. Derived mesh coordinates do not become authoritative saved model geometry.

Highlight Pair restores the latest assembled presentation, removes isolation/ghost filters and clears incompatible picks before showing both occurrence bounds and the optional approximate witness segment. Select A/B selects the corresponding current evaluated face with a checked vertex from that face's index range. It does not turn a witness estimate or a bounding-box center into a fictitious exact source point. A result action requires the current source fingerprint, full session request identity, matching report contract, inspection identity and root configuration.

## Editing, persistence and work limits

The task-specific docked controls edit the shared document draft: scope, mode, configuration binding, requested clearance, native Boolean tolerance, name and suppression. Quantities support expressions in the effective owning configuration. Invalid expressions block current validation; unsupported pair geometry remains visible without rejecting the whole saved inspection. Advanced Source remains repairable after malformed JSON. Preview validates source before dispatch; Apply requires the current validated evaluation and records one undoable change. Cancel restores the starting shared document checkpoint. Definitions persist in native local files and local Git snapshots; component/library identity remapping preserves paths and configuration references.

Source validation bounds targets to 64 distinct paths of at most 64 IDs, each ID at most 256 bytes, at most 256 KiB of target source per inspection, and at most 4,096 bytes per authored quantity expression. Runtime reported coordinate values, placed native vertices and occurrence translations must be finite and within ±1e12 mm. Boolean tolerance is 0.000001–1 mm; requested clearance is finite and nonnegative.

Runtime limits are shared across all active inspections in the current assembly evaluation:

- Occurrence indexing admits at most 100,000 assembled leaves; lookup, face metadata and selected-path work share 16 million units. Duplicate full paths remain ambiguous, and incomplete indexing never establishes uniqueness.
- One occurrence admits at most 256 solid bodies. Native source and common-result admission precedes geometry copying or validation, with at most 4,096 faces and 16,384 edges per body; aggregate admission allows 32,768 faces, 131,072 edges, 1,048,576 controls and 32 million estimated predicate-work units. A carrier preflight is bounded to degree 32 and nesting depth 16; unsupported or unbounded classes are rejected.
- Cached bounds are computed once per transformed body. Native body/hull comparison permits 65,536 body pairs, 4,096 Boolean calls and one million accumulated native face pairs. Native support/contact proofs share four million charged predicate/control units, at most 262,144 controls per admitted carrier, Bernstein degree at most 64 for winding subdivision, and at most 24 exact subdivision levels. Native source admission applies its tighter degree-32 limit before this work.
- Placed boundary data permits 500,000 triangles, 32,768 face records per occurrence, 16 million hierarchy/triangle comparisons and 131,072 pending node pairs. Cached reference metadata is limited to 32 MiB.
- Aggregate reports allow 64 MiB including occurrences, inactive targets, pairs and diagnostic/object overhead. Pair diagnostics are bounded and deduplicated. Exhaustion reports omitted work instead of asserting an all-clear result.

The current implementation does not provide certified native minimum-clearance distance, general nonplanar tangent/coincident-contact classification, combined overlapping common-volume unions, hole-alignment inspection, dynamic collision sweeps or interactive acceptance evidence. Compilation and lint integration are managed by the parent task; no tests, probes or UI acceptance runs are part of this implementation work.
