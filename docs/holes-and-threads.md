# Designed holes and cosmetic threads

Hole, Counterbore, Countersink and Tapped Hole share one docked editor and the ordinary feature draft. Select a planar model face to start a position sketch, place and constrain its native sketch points, select the intended points, and finish the sketch. The requested hole editor opens with those stable point identities. Existing position sketches can also be selected explicitly. Profile corners are never selected automatically. Changing a target body or position sketch remains part of the preview; removed or suppressed centers require an explicit replacement.

The application-owned drilled-hole contract version 2 retains the target solid output, native position sketch, 1–1000 explicit point IDs, a simple/counterbore/countersink head, bore end condition, reverse sketch-normal entry and signed entry offset. Bore dimensions, head dimensions and countersink included angle remain unit-aware source expressions evaluated in f64. Blind bores have flat bottoms. Through All obtains a conservative exact native control-hull bound and extends the cutter beyond it. The supported convex planar stock path crops exact circular station wires to a proven entry and parallel exit and shares those wires with the new cavity; other stock uses the validated native Boolean path.

Tapped Hole adds optional `thread` intent to the same feature, and the Cosmetic Thread checkbox can enable it on an existing simple, counterbored or countersunk hole. The saved `CosmeticThread` stores explicit nominal diameter, pitch, tap-drill diameter, handedness, depth and Blind/Through All end condition. The kernel constructs the exact tap-drill bore. Nominal diameter and pitch describe the cosmetic thread; they do not create a modeled helix, screw surface or teeth. When thread intent is present, its tap-drill diameter controls geometry and the ordinary unthreaded diameter stays saved as an inactive value. Disabling the thread restores that unthreaded diameter.

All active dimensions must be finite and positive. Nominal diameter must exceed the tap-drill diameter. A counterbore or countersink must accommodate the nominal diameter and leave cylindrical bore length. Thread depth starts at the cylindrical entry below the head and cannot exceed the requested bore length remaining below that head. Through-thread intent requires a Through All bore. Right and left hand are explicit saved choices. Thread depth describes authored manufacturing intent; this cosmetic workflow does not model material engagement, tool motion or thread strength. General thread-profile geometry and modeled threads remain separate engineering work.

The grouped commands, source and point selectors, bore/head/thread fields and semantic actions use the shared persistent-worker feature preview. Apply waits for the current validated evaluation and commits one undoable operation. Cancel restores the source/output checkpoint and clears deferred hole actions. A deferred action retains its document, part, selected body and final explicit point selection; it cannot be reused in another document or part. Pinned library components require Make Unique. Source choices and suppression labels follow the effective configuration, and generated suppressed sketch points cannot serve as hole centers.

Hole Callout dimensions resolve the selected component configuration and designed feature. The exact selected circular model edge must retain that feature's construction producer, match its tap-drill or head radius and lie on an authored center axis. Missing or ambiguous references and unsupported implicit intersection curves fail explicitly. A threaded callout emits configured count, nominal diameter, pitch, RH/LH, thread depth or THRU, tap-drill diameter, bore depth/end and any counterbore/countersink dimensions. It identifies the thread as cosmetic. The primary dimensional tolerance applies to nominal diameter for a threaded callout and bore diameter for an unthreaded callout.

Threaded drawing callouts also emit a 270-degree nominal-diameter mark at the source-defined cylindrical thread entry, projected from the solved center and current component placement. Its three quarter arcs form an exact positive-weight rational drawing curve. PDF display tessellation is presentation only; drawing DXF retains the exact rational spline. This annotation does not become a model edge or face. Native files preserve all thread intent. STEP and ordinary profile DXF contain real bore/stock geometry; they do not add cosmetic helical surfaces or claim thread-feature interchange.

No thread standard, fit class, tap-drill recommendation or nominal designation is inferred. Defaults are editable example dimensions rather than standards data. Standards tables, drill tips, advanced hole stacks, external cosmetic threads, modeled internal/external threads, studs and assembly hole series remain gaps. Existing drilled-hole records without `thread` deserialize unchanged, and the absent field is omitted on serialization. Legacy `hole` operations remain readable. No tests, probes or interactive acceptance exercises were performed; compilation and linting are handled by the integration workflow.
